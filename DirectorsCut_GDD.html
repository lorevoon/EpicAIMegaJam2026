Director's Cut: Love in the Ruins

You are directing a romance film in the middle of a zombie apocalypse. Read the storyboard, study the rehearsal, rig the set with as many cameras as you need, then cut the take live. Capture every scene of the love story, and keep every zombie out of the movie.

GDD v2.0Updated 12 Nov 2026Unreal Engine 5.8 · WindowsDeadline Mon 16 Nov 10:00 HKT
Vision

A love story, framed around the end of the world

The studio wants a romance. The location is crawling with zombies. Your job is to find the angles that tell the love story and hide the apocalypse. Teardown inspires the rhythm: unlimited, calm preparation, then one committed, unforgiving take where the plan meets reality.

Learn the scene

The storyboard says what happens and in what order, never where or when. The rehearsal shows you the rest, at your own pace.

Frame out the dead

Every second a zombie is visible on the live camera costs points. Walls, a burned-out bus and a kiosk are your best friends.

Cut it live

Only the live camera records. With scenes overlapping, choosing when to cut is the core decision of the game.

Camera rigs

As many cameras as you want, three kinds

Static

On a floor stand (40–320 cm), a wall or a ceiling. Its position is fixed after placement. Aim and zoom only.

Dolly

Lay rail points on any floor, wall or ceiling, then mount the camera. Rails cannot pass through scenery. Travel at an adjustable speed (base 600 cm/s).

Crane

Stands on the floor; the boom raises the lens from 40 to 800 cm. Great for looking over the bus and the barricade.

Whatever position and aim a camera has when you leave it during preparation is where it starts every take. Zoom runs from 20° to 90° horizontal field of view; zooming in extends the distance at which scenes still count.

Capture rules

What counts as filming a scene, and what counts as a zombie in shot

A scene is captured when

  • its time window is running and the camera is the live one;
  • every subject (head, prop or body) sits inside the central 70% of the frame;
  • each subject is within range (2,000 cm, more when zoomed) and not blocked by scenery;
  • face-required subjects are seen from the front;
  • all of that holds continuously for the scene's hold time (1.5–2.5 s). Cutting away resets the hold.

A zombie is in shot when

  • its head or body is anywhere in the live camera's full frame;
  • within 4,000 cm and not hidden behind scenery.

Penalty: −150 points per second, with a red frame alarm.

The replay plays back exactly what went out live: every cut, aim, zoom and dolly move, recorded 30 times a second, with letterboxing and the recording look. Zombie moments flash a small red marker on the replay's timeline so players learn where they slipped.

Presentation

Low-poly, golden hour, home-movie romance

  • Art: low-poly 3D asset packs (originally made for Unity) imported into Unreal: two survivors, a zombie set, and a ruined-town kit. Check each pack's licence allows use in Unreal and in a public jam build, and list it in the credits.
  • Recording effect: REC dot and timecode, letterbox bars, light grain and vignette on the live feed; a short whip or flash on each cut.
  • Audio: ambience, distant groans, clapperboard, "Action!" and "Cut!", a romantic theme that plays under the replay.
  • HUD: phase, take clock, camera switcher with type labels, scene gauges for the live camera, zombie alarm. Required scene in gold, zombies in green, live camera in red.

Acceptance tests

Done means a player can do this

  1. Read the storyboard, rehearse at 2× and paused, and scrub to the kiss.
  2. Place one camera of each type; set a dolly's start and a crane's height; see them restored after a retake.
  3. Capture the kiss only: the grade is C, Studio Cut.
  4. Leave a zombie on the live camera for 2 s: exactly 300 points are lost and the alarm shows.
  5. Cut away halfway through a scene: its hold resets.
  6. Miss the kiss: "Retake required", no best score saved.
  7. Watch the replay: every cut and camera move matches the take.
  8. Retake ten times in a row: actors, zombies, clock and scores reset; cameras stay.
  9. The packaged build runs outside the editor at 60 FPS at 1080p and exits cleanly.
Open questions
