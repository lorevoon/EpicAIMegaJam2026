FILM CREW OUTFITS V2 - CURRENT REVISED EXPORTS

This folder contains the revised five rigged FBX and GLB characters.
Editable scene: ../FilmCrew_Outfits_v2.blend
Lineup preview: ../FilmCrew_Outfits_v2.png
Rear inspection: ../Outfit_v2_Back.png

Changes from V1:
- Connected T-shirt topology with actual neckline, hem and two cuff openings.
- Subdivided, smoothly shaded cloth; 1.2 mm thickness and mild modeled folds.
- Blended shoulder/spine skin weights; original skeleton and bone names.
- Relaxed, straight trouser legs with knee/ankle creases and 1.5 mm thickness.
- Removed original fantasy ties and sculpted underwear details.
- Rounded black work sneakers: separate rubber sole, upper, padded collar,
  conforming tongue, crossed laces, and toe stitching.
- Characters' heads, hair, caps, headsets and megaphones retained.

Created and reviewed through the live Blender MCP connection.
The clothes use modeled folds, not a live cloth simulation. No physics cache
or cloth simulation is required in the target game engine.

Five individual exports are at the origin, in the original rest pose.
Each retains the original 99-bone skeleton. The Blender scene is arranged
and posed for viewing only. No animation clips or engine prefabs are included.
Review animation retargeting and clothing clearance with your game's clips.

Research applied:
Blender Solidify modifier - thin surface thickness:
https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.0/modeling/modifiers/generate/solidify.html
Blender Subdivision Surface - smoothing the garment shape:
https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/generate/subdivision_surface.html
Nabesaka - cloth finishing with sub-millimetre solidify and subdivision:
https://nabesaka.com/blog/controlling-cloth-simulations-in-blender/
Julien Kaspar / Blender Studio - modeling persistent clothing folds:
https://studio.blender.org/training/stylized-character-workflow/5e70a5b3db0e8bc0c8b226ec/
Blender Secrets - body-derived pants basemesh, topology and pant folds:
https://blender-secrets-school.teachable.com/p/anime-character-modeling-in-blender
Derek Elliott - separate shoe base, sole, upper trim and details:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DncP3mKJB2M

Research used accessible descriptions and text. Paywalled lesson videos
were not accessed or represented as watched. No third-party model was used.
