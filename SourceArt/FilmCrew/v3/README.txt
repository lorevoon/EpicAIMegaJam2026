Five individual FBX and GLB files in this folder. Original hero pack files were not overwritten.

Garment revisions: fitted thin T-shirts (0.65 mm solidify), gravity drape baked into garment vertices, relaxed small creases, straight trouser legs (0.9 mm thickness), localized knee/cuff shaping and softened hip transitions. Original caps, headsets, megaphones and shoes retained.

Skeleton verification: all five Blender armatures match fresh original hero imports exactly in bone names, count (99), rest matrices, heads, tails, parents and connection/deformation flags. No rest bones changed. FBX reimport world bind matrices match the originals to within 0.000000835; parent relationships match. See ../rig_audit_v3.json and export_skeleton_audit.json.
All five exported FBX files were reimported and have zero unweighted vertices. GLB headers and skins verified. This does not constitute testing every animation in Unity.
